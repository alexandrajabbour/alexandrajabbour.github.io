I am an Assistant professor of Political science at the University of Warwick. Previously, I was a Max Weber Fellow and Part-time Assistant professor in quantitative methods at the European University Institute. I held visiting positions at Sciences Po and Aarhus University.
I am interested in how individuals react politically to major structural shifts in society, such as changes in the housing market, social norms, or evolving living arrangements.
In my research, I employ mostly quantitative methods and survey experiments, with an interest in measurement.
My scholarship is published in the Journal of Politics (in-principle acceptance), the European Journal of Political Research, Party Politics, and Electoral Studies.
I received my PhD in 2023 from the University of Montreal, where I also received my BA in Political Science.
I am happy to provide advice or mentoring to students looking for postgraduate studies, especially those from underrepresented backgrounds in academia. Just drop me an email at alexandra.jabbour [at] warwick.ac.uk.
